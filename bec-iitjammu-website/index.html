Who we are

Not an exception. An expectation.

BEC is the official student-run entrepreneurship club (E-Cell) of IIT Jammu, dedicated to nurturing the entrepreneurial spirit of the youth and the rapidly growing E-culture in J&K.

We were founded to build a culture of ideation, innovation and venture-building among students — a place where curiosity is encouraged, ideas are argued about honestly, and students are guided towards turning concepts into solutions people actually use.

Our vision is simple: to make entrepreneurship a natural career path for IIT Jammu students. As the institute accelerates its own startup ecosystem through deep-tech incubation, industry MoUs and research commercialisation, BEC aims to be the front door that brings students into that ecosystem early — with the mindset, the network and the confidence to build.

The ecosystem

Idea to execution, without leaving campus

BEC works closely with IIT Jammu's growing innovation ecosystem, so a student with an idea is never more than one introduction away from real support.

I3C

Institute Innovation and Incubation Council. The institute's incubation arm — a not-for-profit set up to back the entrepreneurial pursuits of student and regional founders.

I2EDC

Innovation, Incubation & Entrepreneurship Development Cell. Drives entrepreneurship development, training and innovation programming across IIT Jammu.

AIC

Atal Incubation Centre. Supported under AIM, NITI Aayog — labs, co-working space, prototyping support and mentor access for early-stage teams.

ACIC

Atal Community Innovation Centre. Also under AIM, NITI Aayog — focused on community-rooted innovation across Jammu & Kashmir.

Beyond campus

A network that keeps growing

BEC is building active working relationships with schools and close to a dozen higher-education institutions — including IIMs, NITs and IITs — and the list keeps expanding as we bring in collaborators for events, competitions and outreach.

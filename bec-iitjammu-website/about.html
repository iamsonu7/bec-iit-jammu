About BEC

Budding Entrepreneurs' Club is the official student-run entrepreneurship club of IIT Jammu — dedicated to nurturing the entrepreneurial spirit of the youth and the rapidly growing E-culture in Jammu & Kashmir.

Our story

Formed around a simple idea

That students should be able to explore an idea seriously, long before they are ready to call it a company.

The Budding Entrepreneurs' Club was formed with the vision of fostering innovation and entrepreneurial thinking among students, by creating opportunities to explore ideas, collaborate, and learn from mentors and industry experts. The club serves as a platform where curiosity is encouraged, ideas are discussed, and students are guided towards transforming their concepts into meaningful solutions.

BEC was founded to build a culture of ideation, innovation and venture-building among students. We work closely with IIT Jammu's growing innovation ecosystem to give students a launchpad from idea to execution — and we organise workshops, speaker sessions, startup bootcamps, case study challenges and networking events that expose students to the real world of building and scaling ventures.

“Our vision is simple: to make entrepreneurship a natural career path for IIT Jammu students — not an exception, but an expectation.”

Budding Entrepreneurs' Club, IIT Jammu

Vision & mission

What we're actually trying to do

Our vision

To make entrepreneurship a natural career path for IIT Jammu students — not an exception, but an expectation — and to be the front door that brings students into the institute's innovation ecosystem early.

Our mission

To equip students with the mindset, network and confidence to build — through consistent programming, honest mentorship, and a route from a rough idea to a working prototype and beyond.

Our values

Curiosity over credentials. Honest feedback over polite applause. Doing over discussing. And a community where the first idea is allowed to be a bad one.

Positioning

Like an E-Cell — with a J&K mandate

BEC is the official student-run entrepreneurship club of IIT Jammu, and it plays the role an E-Cell plays at other institutes. What makes it different is where it sits: at the centre of a young, fast-growing entrepreneurial ecosystem in Jammu & Kashmir, at an institute that is itself accelerating deep-tech incubation, industry MoUs and research commercialisation.

That means our work isn't only inward-facing. Alongside campus programming, we run outreach into schools and other campuses across the region — reaching young minds early, before anyone has told them entrepreneurship is risky.

  • Student-run. Every programme is designed and delivered by students.
  • Ecosystem-connected. Direct lines into I3C, I2EDC, AIC and ACIC.
  • Regionally rooted. Building E-culture across Jammu & Kashmir, not just on campus.
  • Nationally networked. Working with IITs, IIMs, NITs and schools.
  • Open by default. You don't need a startup, a team, or a plan to walk in.
The IIT Jammu ecosystem

Who does what, and when you'd talk to them

BEC is the student entry point. These are the bodies we work with to take a student idea further than a club alone could.

I3C — Institute Innovation and Incubation Council

The institute's incubation arm and a not-for-profit body set up to fuel the entrepreneurial pursuits of founders from Jammu, Kashmir and beyond. Where a student venture goes when it is ready for formal incubation support.

I2EDC — Innovation, Incubation & Entrepreneurship Development Cell

Drives entrepreneurship development and innovation programming at IIT Jammu — training, institutional support and the frameworks that keep the ecosystem running.

AIC — Atal Incubation Centre

Supported under the Atal Innovation Mission, NITI Aayog. Gives early-stage startups access to state-of-the-art labs, co-working space, prototyping facilities and industry mentors.

ACIC — Atal Community Innovation Centre

Also under the Atal Innovation Mission, NITI Aayog. Focused on community-rooted innovation — bringing innovation capacity to underserved parts of the region.

How a student moves through BEC

From a hunch to a handoff

There's no single entry point — but this is the path most people end up walking.

  1. Step 01

    Show up

    Attend a speaker session or workshop. No commitment, no application — just turn up and see whether this is your kind of room.

  2. Step 02

    Drop an idea

    Use the Idea Dropbox. Rough is fine — a paragraph is enough. The team reads it and comes back to you with a next step.

  3. Step 03

    Test it in public

    Enter a case challenge or a bootcamp. Get your thinking pulled apart by people who want it to work.

  4. Step 04

    Join the Big Idea Cohort

    Structured mentorship over several weeks: talk to users, narrow the problem, build the smallest useful thing, and demo it.

  5. Step 05

    Hand off to the ecosystem

    When it's ready, we introduce you to I3C, AIC or ACIC — labs, seed support, incubation and investor connects.

Come build with us.

Recruitment, the Big Idea Cohort and the Idea Dropbox are all open doors — pick whichever one fits where you are right now.

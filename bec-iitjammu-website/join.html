Why join

What you actually get out of it

BEC isn't a line on a CV. It's a few years of running real events with real budgets, talking to founders who don't have to be polite to you, and finding out whether building things is something you want to do with your life.

  • Ownership from day one. Members run sessions, not just attend them.
  • Direct access to mentors — founders, operators and investors in our network.
  • A route into the ecosystem via I3C, I2EDC, AIC and ACIC.
  • A national network across IITs, IIMs, NITs and other E-Cells.
  • Skills that transfer — negotiation, sponsorship, design, ops, public speaking.
  • People who take your idea seriously before anybody else does.
Verticals

Where you could fit

Pick the one that sounds like you. If two of them do, say so in the application — most members end up working across more than one.

Operations & Events

Plan and run everything: workshops, bootcamps, speaker sessions, competitions. Logistics, timelines, and making sure the room is actually full.

Outreach & Partnerships

Build the network. Schools, other campuses, IITs, IIMs, NITs and student E-Cells — the relationships that make our events bigger than us.

Design & Creatives

Posters, decks, brand, social creatives, event identity. If you have opinions about type and colour, this is your vertical.

Tech & Web

Keep this website alive, build tools for events, handle registrations and data. Front-end, back-end or just willing to learn.

Content & Media

Write the handbooks, run the socials, cover the events, interview the speakers. Words, photos and video.

Finance & Sponsorship

Budgets, sponsor decks, cold outreach and closing deals. The vertical where you learn to ask strangers for money.

The process

How recruitment works

No aptitude test, no GPA cutoff. We're looking for people who follow through.

  1. Step 01

    Fill the application

    A short form: who you are, which vertical interests you, and one thing you've built, organised or genuinely tried.

  2. Step 02

    A short task

    Something small and relevant to your vertical — a design, a plan, a piece of writing, a bit of research. A few hours, not a few weeks.

  3. Step 03

    Conversation

    An informal chat with current members. We'll ask about your task and what you'd want to run if you joined.

  4. Step 04

    Onboarding

    You're in. You get the Member Handbook, a vertical, and a real responsibility on the next event.

Initiatives & events

Workshops, speaker sessions, startup bootcamps, case study challenges and networking events — plus two flagship programmes designed to carry an idea forward.

Flagship programme

Big Idea Cohort

A structured programme for students who want to stop describing an idea and start testing it.

Over the course of the cohort you'll narrow a problem, talk to the people who have it, build the smallest thing that answers your riskiest question, and defend the result in front of mentors and peers. It runs alongside the institute's incubation ecosystem, so teams that are ready can be handed straight to I3C, AIC or ACIC.

  • Weekly mentor check-ins with founders and industry experts
  • Structured milestones — problem, users, prototype, pitch
  • Peer review sessions with other teams in the cohort
  • Demo day in front of the ecosystem and invited guests
  • A direct route into incubation support for teams that are ready
Apply to the cohort
How it runs
  1. Phase 1

    Problem & users

    Pick a problem worth solving and go talk to the people who actually have it.

  2. Phase 2

    Solution & prototype

    Build the smallest version that produces real feedback, not applause.

  3. Phase 3

    Traction & model

    Test whether anyone wants it, and what a business around it would look like.

  4. Phase 4

    Demo & handoff

    Pitch to mentors and the ecosystem — and take the next step with incubation support.

What happens after you submit

  1. A member of the team reads it — every submission gets read.
  2. We come back to you with an honest first reaction and questions.
  3. We point you to the right next step: a mentor, a cohort, a competition, or a co-founder.
  4. If it has legs, we help you get it in front of the ecosystem.
Always open

Idea Dropbox

The lowest-friction way to start. No pitch deck, no team, no plan required.

Most good ventures start as an offhand observation that someone didn't throw away. The Idea Dropbox exists so that observation has somewhere to go. Write a paragraph. That's genuinely enough — we'd rather see it early and rough than polished and too late.

Open to every student at IIT Jammu, all year round, at any stage of an idea.

Our formats

Five things we run, again and again

Different entry points for different kinds of students — and different stages of an idea.

Workshops

Practical, hands-on sessions: customer validation, product thinking, unit economics, financial modelling, go-to-market and pitching. You leave having made something, not just heard something.

Speaker sessions

Founders, operators and investors talking about what actually happened — the pivots, the fundraising that didn't close, the hire that went wrong. Followed by open Q&A.

Startup bootcamps

Multi-day sprints. Teams form on day one, find a problem, build something demonstrable, and pitch it before the weekend is over. The fastest way to find out whether you like building.

Case study challenges

Real business problems with real constraints, judged by people from industry. Competitions run both on campus and jointly with partner institutes across the country.

Networking events

Mixers, founder dinners and ecosystem meetups that put students in the same room as mentors, alumni, incubator teams and other student E-Cells.

Outreach programmes

Sessions in schools and campuses across Jammu & Kashmir and beyond, run with our partner institutions — reaching young minds before anyone tells them not to try.
